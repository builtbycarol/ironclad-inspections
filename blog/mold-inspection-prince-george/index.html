Mold Inspection & Testing in Prince George

By Mike Webber · Ironclad Inspections · Prince George, BC · August 2026

Musty smell that won't go away. A patch of discoloration on a ceiling or in a closet corner. Family members with symptoms that never seem to have a clear cause. Mold is one of the few home issues you genuinely cannot eyeball your way to certainty on — you either test it, or you're guessing. This guide covers what mold exposure can do to your health, how testing actually works, and why the person inspecting your air shouldn't be the same one selling you the cleanup.

Could Mold Be Behind How You're Feeling?

Mold exposure doesn't always announce itself. Sometimes it's an obvious smell or visible growth. Often it shows up instead as symptoms that are easy to blame on something else — a cold that won't quite clear, allergies that seem worse at home than anywhere else, a cough that started around the same time you noticed that damp spot in the basement.

Symptoms Worth Paying Attention To

  • Ongoing cough or wheezing
  • Shortness of breath
  • Stuffy nose or sinus pressure
  • Sore or scratchy throat
  • Itchy, watery, or red eyes
  • Skin irritation or rashes
  • Trouble concentrating or "brain fog"
  • Recurring lung or sinus infections
  • Fatigue and headaches that won't quit
  • New asthma symptoms in kids

"If any of this sounds familiar, especially if symptoms ease up when you're away from home, hidden mold is worth ruling in or out. Testing is the only way to actually know — not guess."

What Mold Exposure Can Actually Do

Mold isn't just a cosmetic problem. The research on prolonged exposure points to a real range of health effects, which is exactly why guessing isn't good enough.

💨

Respiratory Irritation

Mold spores in the air are a known trigger for coughing, wheezing, and worsened asthma symptoms.

🫁

Lung Inflammation

Heavy, prolonged exposure has been linked to hypersensitivity pneumonitis — inflammation of the lungs from inhaling contaminated dust.

⚠️

Black Mold Sensitivity

Certain species, including black mold, can produce more pronounced reactions in people with allergies or weakened immune systems.

👃

Sinus Problems

Chronic sinusitis and recurring sinus infections are commonly tied to ongoing mold exposure.

🧒

Childhood Asthma Risk

Early-life exposure to mold is considered a risk factor for children later developing asthma.

😩

Fatigue & Headaches

Living with unresolved mold exposure is associated with ongoing fatigue and headaches that don't have another obvious cause.

What Happens During Your Inspection

  1. Visual walkthrough of the home, checking areas prone to moisture — bathrooms, basements, crawlspaces, attics, and around windows.
  2. Infrared thermal scanning to flag hidden moisture behind walls and ceilings before it's visible.
  3. Air samples collected indoors, plus one outdoor control sample for comparison.
  4. Samples sent to an independent lab — results back within 48 hours.
  5. You get a full written report, and I walk you through exactly what it means and what your next steps should be.

If mold is found, the next step is identifying and fixing the moisture source, then hiring a remediation company to remove it. I'm happy to point you toward reputable remediation contractors in the area — but I don't do that work myself, and I don't get a referral fee for the introduction either.

Why This Matters in Prince George

Cold winters, freeze-thaw cycling, and homes that trap humidity through long heating seasons all add up to real conditions for hidden moisture and mold, especially in crawlspaces, attics, and older homes without great ventilation. A musty smell, a past leak that was "dried out," or unexplained symptoms in the household are all good reasons to get an actual answer instead of wondering.

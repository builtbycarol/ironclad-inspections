Got Questions?

Common Questions About Our Services

What's the difference between a Pre-Purchase and Pre-Listing inspection?

A Pre-Purchase Inspection is ordered by the buyer to evaluate a home before closing. A Pre-Listing Inspection is ordered by the seller before the home goes on the market, so repairs can be managed on their terms instead of under negotiation pressure.

When should I book a Pre-Drywall Inspection?

Before the walls are closed up — this is the only window to visually verify the structure, plumbing, electrical, and mechanical rough-ins before they're covered.

Do you inspect condos and townhouses the same way as detached homes?

Yes — every property type follows the same rigorous "Inside & Out" standard, adjusted for what applies to that property (for example, common-area considerations on condos and shared-wall assessment on townhouses).

Can I combine a mold inspection with a Pre-Purchase Inspection?

Yes — many clients book both together. Call Mike at (250) 649-5522 or mention it when booking online.

How long does an inspection take?

Most inspections take 3.5–4.5 hours depending on the size and condition of the property, followed by a personal 30-minute walkthrough.

When will I get my report?

You'll receive your detailed digital Spectora report the same day, often before the evening is out.

What areas do you serve?

Prince George, Quesnel, and Vanderhoof — approximately 1 to 1.5 hours out from Prince George. Call (250) 649-5522 to confirm coverage of your property.

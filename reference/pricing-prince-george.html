Got Questions?

Common Pricing Questions

How much does a home inspection cost?

Pricing starts at $350 for condos and goes up based on property size — most standard homes are $600. Townhouses are $450. See the full breakdown above, or call (250) 649-5522 for a quick quote.

What determines the price of my inspection?

Price is based primarily on the square footage and type of property (condo, townhouse, or detached home). Larger homes take longer to inspect thoroughly, which is reflected in the pricing tiers.

Are there any hidden fees or add-ons?

No. Thermal imaging, pest inspection, appliance inspection, and your same-day report are all included at no extra cost. The only separate line item is lab-tested mold air sampling at a flat $550.

Do you offer a discount for booking a mold inspection with a home inspection?

Call Mike at (250) 649-5522 to ask about bundling — many clients book both together during the same visit.

What if my property is larger than 6,500 sq ft?

Larger homes, acreages, and properties with secondary structures (shops, guest houses, etc.) may require a custom quote. Call (250) 649-5522 for accurate pricing.

How do I get an exact quote?

Use the instant quote tool on the main site, call (250) 649-5522, or book online and confirm final pricing with Mike directly.
